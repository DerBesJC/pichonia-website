# Engineering Services (v1.1)

Pichonia is an engineering company that ships durable systems—our own products and scoped client builds—under the same reliability bar.

## Service lines
- **Product engineering** — Discovery → UX/UI → Mobile/Web → APIs → QA → Launch  
  *Flutter & web apps, payments, auth, analytics, accessibility, performance.*
- **AI & data engineering** — Data pipelines, feature stores, model integration & eval, MLOps, monitoring & guardrails.  
  *Practical AI: signals, retrieval, evaluation, privacy by default.*
- **Logistics systems** — Marketplaces, dispatch, routing, real-time tracking, driver apps, pricing, billing, compliance.  
  *End-to-end flows like Dropt: matching, booking, tracking, settlements.*
- **Cloud, security & compliance** — Infra (GCP/AWS), IaC, CI/CD, observability, secrets, policies & SLAs.  
  *SOC2-ready controls; clear separation of environments and access.*

## Working style
Scoped sprints, plain-English SOWs & SLAs, evidence over claims. We align on outcomes, timeframes, and economics before we ship.
