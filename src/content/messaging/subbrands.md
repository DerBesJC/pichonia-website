## Dropt — Logistics

Dropt is Pichonia’s logistics platform. It connects shippers with verified truck owners and drivers, so matching, booking, and tracking stay simple.

- Marketplace for road logistics (trucks, drivers, shippers)
- Matching, pricing, routing, tracking, and settlements
- Mobile-first experience for both operators and drivers

## Derbest AI — Markets

Derbest AI turns messy market data into actionable signals for operators and traders.

- Data pipelines, feature engineering, and model integration
- Focus on transparency, evaluation, and guardrails over hype
- Built for real decision-making, not vanity dashboards

Both sit under **Pichonia LLC** and follow the same reliability bar, privacy posture, and policy stack at **policies.pichonia.com**.
