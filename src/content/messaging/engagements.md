We keep engagements scoped and testable so everyone can see whether we’re on track.

## Common engagement shapes

- **Discovery & strategy sprints**  
  Clarify the problem, stakeholders, constraints, and opportunities. Produce a concrete brief, roadmap, and architecture that can actually be built.

- **Build & launch**  
  Ship a specific product or feature set: UX/UI, implementation, testing, launch, and early support.

- **Stabilize & optimize**  
  Take an existing system and make it reliable: fix core issues, add monitoring, improve performance, simplify operations.

- **Longer-term partnerships**  
  Ongoing work across product, engineering, AI, and infra with clear scopes and renewal points.

## How we work

- Plain-English SOWs and SLAs  
- Evidence over claims (demos, metrics, diffs)
- Regular check-ins, written updates, and clear owners
- Clean handovers so you can run what we build
